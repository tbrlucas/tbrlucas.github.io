About this site

This is a project about making older devices useful, interesting and enjoyable again.

Phones and tablets do not become useless just because newer models arrive. There are still apps to discover, features to explore and different ways to give an old device a purpose.

The purpose

The goal is to collect clear recommendations and practical ideas for devices that are no longer part of everyday use.

The content is intended to help visitors understand their options, explore what their device can do and decide which ideas are worth trying.

What the project covers

iPhone and iPad
Apps, settings, useful features and ideas for older Apple devices.

Android
Recommendations for older Android phones and tablets, from familiar models to devices that have been sitting unused.

Practical guides
Explanations and suggestions written to be approachable, with the relevant context instead of unnecessary complexity.

A few principles

Free to read. The site does not charge visitors to access its content.

No advertising. The project is intended to be a straightforward resource, without ad placements.

Responsible use. The focus is on legitimate, everyday uses for devices—not hacking or illegal activity.
